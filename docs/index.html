A ten round game of trust

What could possibly go wrong?

A little game about how we treat each other when there’s something to gain. We count coins, but they could just as easily be kisses. Play to the end and see what you’re both left with.

Sit together or use a call. Each team uses one phone and chooses together.

Together, both teams

Both cooperate: +3 coins each · Both defect: −3 coins each

Rounds 9 and 10 double the coins won or lost.
Below zero means a loss.

Rage quit?
